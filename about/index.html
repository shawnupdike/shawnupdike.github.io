CAREER EXPERIENCE

Everyone gets their start somewhere, for myself it was at 365 Parking. My role was to create content for their socials to boost traffic and conversions.

Towards the end of my academia, I found my way working at ISlide USA. I was on a team of designers, in charge of proof requests for custom slides.

Eventually landing gigs as a Motion Graphic Designer at ParsonsKellogg & a Web Designer at Black Door Creative. These gigs pushed my career to new heights with increased skillsets & knowledge.

On the side I was hired as a Junior Graphic Designer at Rocketbook & CertaPro Painters to assist with routine daily tasks respectfully for their business needs.

Today I am a Web Designer at IGT, focusing on in-house requests for new web page builds, maintenance of standing site & sub domains, as well as front-end development for site components & backend server patches.
