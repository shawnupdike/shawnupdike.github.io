Magic of Festivals

The Bandemic microsite was created to showcase a fictional music festival that takes place post-pandemic.

The design focuses on creating an engaging and immersive experience for users, highlighting the excitement and energy of live music events.

The site features vibrant visuals, intuitive navigation, and interactive elements to captivate visitors and encourage them to explore.

Process

Concept was rooted in the idea of putting on a live music event where collective artists from different genres would perform post pandemic that was organized by the Rolling Stones. Wire-frames & testing occurred in Adobe XD & later coded into a working site. The theme is within the name of the festival “Bandemic”, hence we need to band together afterwards together over music and festivities.

  • Ease of navigation
  • Responsive design
  • Interactive elements
  • Immersive visuals
  • Engaging user experience

Final Result

This site has very simple navigation and is completed functional via Javascript and JQuery with a concrete foundation in both HTML and Sass. This web design was a success with the simplistic and edgy branding.
