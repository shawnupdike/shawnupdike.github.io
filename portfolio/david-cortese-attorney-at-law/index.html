Website Reboot

David P. Cortese is an attorney at law based in Massachusetts who needed a website redesign to better represent his legal services and expertise. The goal was to create a modern, professional website that would instill trust and credibility in potential clients seeking legal assistance.

Process

I began with migrating his old website from Bluehost to WordPress hosting. From there photo assets were gathered along with info content describing what the firm’s services were. Font’s, colors & hierarchy got a nice upgrade.

  • Upgrading the website to a modern responsive design that worked on all devices.
  • Created a clean, easy to navigate layout that highlighted the firm’s services and expertise.
  • Implemented SEO best practices to improve the site’s visibility in search engines.

Final Result

The end result was a modern, sleek website that represented the Law Office of David P. Cortese in a professional manner while also giving the user a sense of trust and credibility when seeking legal services in Massachusetts.
