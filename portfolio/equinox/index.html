Project Date: November 2019

JWU EQUINOX

FALL CONCERT

Develop a brand identity for a live concert being put on at Johnson & Wales University. Peripherals that are needed include: posters, stage backdrop, access passes, marketing promos, handbills & stickers. A team of myself and three other talented student designers teamed up with the SEEM class at JWU to put on a fall concert at the JWU Pepsi Forum.

Rock & Roll All Nite!

Each team member was responsible for designing a particular asset that would be used to promote the concert.

After getting the brand identity approved, I went to work creating the posters that would be printed out & distributed around the campus to advertise the event.

As well as a multi poster backdrop that would be hung with pipe & drape at the live concert. Using Illustrator, the brand development that went into these peripherals was both effortless and strategic and solving the design problem.

Data Driven

The design process began with brainstorming sessions with the team to determine the overall look & feel of the brand.

  • Created mood boards to explore different visual styles & aesthetics for the brand.
  • Developed multiple logo concepts & presented them to the team for feedback & refinement.
  • Collaborated with the team to ensure that all peripherals were cohesive & aligned with the overall brand identity.

Grand Finale!

The end result was a huge success, the event had good turnout & the peripherals were effectively used by our client in their tasks for the venue. Our creative team got published by JWU, the blog is available on their site discussing our project roles.

  • Successful Event Execution
  • Completed and Delivered on Time
  • Customer Satisfaction
  • Improved Brand Awareness
