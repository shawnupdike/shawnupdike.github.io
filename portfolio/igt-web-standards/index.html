Creating Something From Nothing

Being tasked with creating a web standards guide from scratch for a large enterprise company required careful planning and execution.

Very daunting being the sole Web Designer on staff.

This project involved extensive research, collaboration with stakeholders, and a deep understanding of web development best practices to ensure the guide was comprehensive and effective.

Extensive Effort

The process of creating the IGT Web Standards guide involved several key steps to ensure its effectiveness and usability:

  • Process & Workflow Maps
  • Branding & Design Systems
  • Component & Development Standards
  • Security & Accessibility Guidelines

Stepping Stone

The final IGT Web Standards guide received positive feedback from stakeholders and served as a valuable resource for marketing and web departments within the organization. It helped streamline web development processes, improve consistency across web applications, and enhance the overall user experience.

These standards laid the foundation for the future IGT Website Rebuild project, more to come on that later.
